Privacy Policy

SmartScan Pro is designed as a local-first barcode, inventory, tool tracking, and point-of-sale application. This policy explains what information the app accesses, what stays on your device, and when limited information may be sent to a third-party service.

Information stored on your device

Inventory records, product notes, quantities, prices, sales records, tool assignments, worker or assignee names, job-site labels, and activity history are stored locally on your device. SmartScan Pro does not require a SmartScan account and does not operate a developer-controlled cloud database for this information.

If you choose to export, print, or back up information, the resulting file is handled by your device and by the destination you choose. Your operating system may also back up application data according to your device or Google account backup settings.

Camera access

SmartScan Pro requests camera permission so you can scan barcodes and QR codes. Camera frames are processed for scanning functionality. SmartScan Pro does not intentionally upload or store camera photos or video on a developer-controlled server.

Product lookup

For supported numeric product barcodes, SmartScan Pro can request public product information from Open Food Facts. When this lookup is used, the barcode value is sent to the Open Food Facts service. The request is configured without credentials and without an HTTP referrer. Open Food Facts may receive ordinary network information associated with the request, such as an IP address, under its own privacy practices.

Data we do not use

  • No SmartScan user account is required.
  • No advertising SDK is included.
  • No developer-controlled analytics or behavioral tracking is included.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, messages, health data, or financial account credentials.

Payments

The point-of-sale feature can record a payment method label such as cash, card, Zelle, or other. SmartScan Pro does not process payment cards and is not a payment processor. Do not enter card numbers, bank credentials, or other sensitive payment credentials into free-text fields.

Data deletion

Because operational data is stored locally, you can delete individual records inside the app or clear the app's local storage/data from your device settings. Uninstalling the app generally removes locally stored app data, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior.

Security

SmartScan Pro minimizes remote data transfer by keeping operational records on the device. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so users should protect access to their device and exported files.

Children

SmartScan Pro is a business and productivity tool and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when SmartScan Pro features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the SmartScan Pro Google Play listing.

This privacy policy describes SmartScan Pro version 0.4.0. If cloud sync, accounts, analytics, advertising, online payments, or additional third-party services are added later, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before release.
