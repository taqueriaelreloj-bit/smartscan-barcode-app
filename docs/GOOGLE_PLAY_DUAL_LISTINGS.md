# Google Play listings — ProServices market test

Google Play current metadata limits: app title 30 characters, short description 80 characters, full description 4,000 characters. Do not place price claims in listing metadata. Prices are configured separately in Play Console.

## App 1 — QR & Barcode Scanner

- Developer: ProServices
- Package ID: `com.proservices.qrbarcodescanner`
- Initial paid price: USD 0.99
- Category suggestion: Tools
- App title: `QR & Barcode Scanner`
- Short description: `Fast QR and barcode scanning with image scan, sharing and local history.`

### Full description

Scan QR codes and common barcodes quickly from your phone camera or an image.

QR & Barcode Scanner by ProServices is designed for people who want a focused scanner without inventory or point-of-sale complexity.

Features include:
- QR and barcode camera scanning
- Scan codes from saved images
- Open detected links safely
- Copy and share scanned values
- Basic scan history stored locally on your device
- Support for common QR, UPC, EAN, Code 39, Code 93, Code 128, ITF, Codabar, Data Matrix, Aztec and PDF417 formats when supported by the device
- No inventory or POS modules in this edition

Your scan history is stored locally on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details about optional external product-data lookups and device permissions.

## App 2 — Barcode Inventory & POS

- Developer: ProServices
- Package ID: `com.proservices.barcodeinventory`
- Initial paid price: USD 4.99
- Category suggestion: Business
- App title: `Barcode Inventory & POS`
- Short description: `Scan barcodes, track stock and assets, record sales and export business data.`

### Full description

Turn your phone into a practical barcode inventory and local point-of-sale tool.

Barcode Inventory & POS by ProServices combines fast scanning with inventory, stock tracking, sales records and asset management for small businesses, contractors, shops and field teams.

Features include:
- QR and barcode camera scanning
- Scan codes from saved images
- Inventory quantities and stock alerts
- Product and location records
- Sales and local POS workflow
- Daily totals and sales history
- Serialized tool and asset tracking
- Checkout and return records for tools and equipment
- CSV export
- Local backup and restore
- Offline-first workflows for supported features

The app is designed to keep core business records local on the device. Optional product information may use external product-data services when requested. See the Privacy Policy for details.

## Pricing rule for launch

Create both apps as **paid** before first publication. Google Play allows a paid app to be changed to free later, but an app that has already been offered for free cannot later be changed back to paid using the same package ID. If the market test changes, adjust the paid price rather than publishing these package IDs as free first.
