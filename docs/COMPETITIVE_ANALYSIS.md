## Product opportunity

Most leading products specialize in one job: decode, shop, explain health data, or manage inventory. SmartScan Pro can occupy the intersection with a modular product:

1. **Scan:** instant, universal, camera/image/manual, batch capable.
2. **Understand:** safe action, product identity, explainable food data, source and confidence.
3. **Decide:** compare, save, watch, choose an alternative.
4. **Track:** personal pantry, tools, materials, retail stock, assets, or job-site inventory.
5. **Automate:** alerts, labels, exports, webhooks, integrations, and team workflows.

## Trust as a differentiator

- No advertisement that can be confused with the scanned result.
- Show the exact decoded destination before opening a link.
- Explain when a risk flag is heuristic, not a guarantee.
- Label external product data and its freshness/source.
- Keep local mode usable without an account.
- Never describe nutrition or ingredient data as medical advice.
- Make export and deletion easy.
